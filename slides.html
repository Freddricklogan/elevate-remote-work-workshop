0:13 · The update shape

Done. Doing. Blocked. By when.

D
Done. What you finished since the last update. Specific: file names, numbers, links.
D
Doing. What you are on now and when it will be done. A date, not "soon."
B
Blocked. What you need and from whom. Blank is fine; "nothing" is a real answer.
Update formulaDone: finished item + link. Doing: current task, by date, time. Blocked: what I need from person, or “nothing.”
