Facilitator guide

Run of show, 45 minutes

This is a working session, not a lecture. Every block ends with students doing something on the handout. Put slides.html full screen on the projector and press P to open the presenter view on your laptop screen: notes, next slide, clock, run of show, and the student question board.

TimeBlockWhat you doAsk the room
12:30DoorsProjector on slide 1. Handout link and check-in QR on screen. Greet; ask each arriving student what team project they are on."What team are you on right now where nobody can see you work?"
0:00The inventorySlide 2. Start the 60-second timer. Students list online collaboration tools. Poll Q1 on phones."Group projects count. Who's done remote work without calling it that?"
0:03Why it mattersSlides 3–4. Roadmap in 30 seconds. 26% of paid days remote (WFH Research, Sept 2026); 51% hybrid (Gallup, Sept 2025)."What's the last message you sent that a stranger could act on without a follow-up?"
0:10Working style + update shapeSlides 5–7. Two minutes of silent drafting (Step 2 on handout). Volunteer reads theirs; type it into the builder. Write one Done / Doing / Blocked out loud. Click the tab for majors in the room."Give me a project. Let's write its update." "Where's the date on your Doing line?"
0:18Know your riskSlide 8. Run the dropdown with two students' answers. The fix is always: write it down first. Poll Q2."What's your remote failure mode?"
0:26The remote day + practiceSlides 9–10. Five moves; check-in is the core. Name the Elevate remote internship (8–10 weeks, for credit or non-credit) and Forage."Name one team you're on right now where nobody can see you work."
0:33Write one updateSlide 11. Start the 8-minute timer. Walk the room; push for dates and links. At 8:00 start the 3-minute swap."Partner: you're the manager. Can you act on this without asking anything?"
0:41Checklist, exit questions + check-inSlides 12–13. Read the checklist fast. Leave the QR up. Students download or email the worksheet and book a one-on-one."Where are you posting your first real update this week?"

If the room goes quiet

  • Write your own Monday update on screen from a real project; ask the room only for the date.
  • Ask for a show of hands on the risk slide; tap the tally so the screen moves.
  • Use the field tabs: "Who's in architecture? Here's yours."

Bring

  • Laptop and adapter, this site loaded (works offline once opened), handout link on the board.
  • Printed handouts for students without a device (handout.html prints cleanly).
  • Teams open on your laptop to show status, channels, and a real update if Wi-Fi allows.
  • Sign-in: the 312 QR on slide 13 records attendance for Elevate credit.

After

  • Record attendance from 312 and log the session for Elevate workshop credit.
  • Reply to any emailed worksheets within two business days with one revision note each.
  • Export the Forms responses; clear the tallies on the poll page before the next session.

Edit the site

All dates, links, and embeds live in assets/config.js. Slide text is in slides.html; each slide is one <section class="slide">. Speaker notes are the NOTES array at the bottom of that file.
